# Big Data Management and Data Warehouse Design

## Project overview

This case study summarises individual coursework completed for ISIT912 Big Data Management at the University of Wollongong. The work focused on translating business and analytical requirements into data-cube designs, dimensional models, and Hive table structures.

## Work completed

### Data-cube design

Designed fact, measure, dimension, and hierarchy structures for four analytical scenarios:

- temperature and humidity observations
- customer shopping baskets
- trip records
- student participation

### Banking data warehouse

Created a conceptual warehouse model for approved loans, covering facts and measures together with customer, branch, teller, loan-type, insurance, location, and time dimensions.

### University warehouse and Hive implementation

- Designed a snowflake schema for student enrolment analysis
- Modelled Student, Subject, School, Degree, and Enrollment entities
- Wrote Hive HQL to create and load internal tables
- Queried the resulting structures to verify loaded data

### Nested and dimensional modelling

- Modelled employee information in zero normal form using array-based project, contribution, and programming-language attributes
- Produced snowflake, denormalised, and star-schema alternatives for hotel-visit analysis
- Compared how the alternatives affect analytical access and redundancy

## Skills demonstrated

- identifying facts, measures, dimensions, and hierarchies
- dimensional modelling
- star and snowflake schema design
- conceptual and logical data modelling
- Hive data definition, loading, and querying
- reasoning about normalisation, denormalisation, and analytical use cases
- documenting technical decisions clearly

## Technologies and academic exposure

**Applied in completed work:** Hive, HQL, SQL concepts, dimensional modelling, UMLet, data-warehouse design.

**Additional course exposure:** Hadoop/HDFS, MapReduce, HBase, Pig, Spark DataFrames, and Spark SQL. These are listed as academic exposure rather than production experience.
